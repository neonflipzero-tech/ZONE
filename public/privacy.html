Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 11, 2026

Welcome to ZONE. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect certain information to provide better services to you:

  • Profile Information: When you sign in using Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Activity Data: We store your mission progress, level, XP, ZoneCoins, and other discipline statistics.
  • Social Data: If you share posts in the Journey feature, we store that content (images/videos and text).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used for the following purposes:

  • Managing your account and syncing your progress across multiple devices.
  • Displaying your rank on the global Leaderboard.
  • Personalizing missions based on your level and chosen path.
  • Improving app features based on anonymous usage statistics.

3. Data Storage and Security

We use Google Firebase services to store your data securely in the cloud. We implement technical security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

4. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Your data is only shared with our infrastructure services (such as Google Firebase) to run the application's functions.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data that we store.
  • Request the deletion of your account and all related data through the settings menu within the app.
  • Update your profile information.

6. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
